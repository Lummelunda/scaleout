Component Library Primitives · Typography

Type Scale

Display — 80px weight 800. Title slides only.
Subtitle — 28px weight 500, primary color. Under display.
Section label — 22px weight 600, primary color. Sub-headings within a slide.
Body — 20px weight 400, dim color. Supporting prose and explanations. Keep to 2–3 lines.
Callout — 22px, left border accent. One key insight per slide maximum.
Caption — 14px muted. Below images or figures only.
Label — 11px uppercase. Header bars and meta info only.
